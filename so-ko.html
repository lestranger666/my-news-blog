Market Explosion: South Korea's KOSPI Index Rockets 18% In Record-Breaking AI Chip Rally

By Financial Desk • July 31, 2026 • 3 min read

Global financial hubs went into absolute shock this trading session as South Korea's benchmark KOSPI index experienced an extraordinary 18% overnight jump, shattering historical single-day records and triggering massive movements across neighboring Asian markets.

An explosive combination of massive institutional short-covering and an unexpected, aggressive influx of global capital targeting AI-related chipmaking infrastructure fueled the historic rally. Heavyweights Samsung and SK Hynix led the charge, reversing recent declines with historic double-digit percentage turnarounds that momentarily buckled local retail brokerage infrastructure.

Trading floors described the scene as pure, unadulterated mania, forcing automated desks globally to rapidly re-adjust risk limits. While regulatory voices urge caution over volatility, this unprecedented trading activity completely rewrites the short-term evaluation limits for Asian semiconductor supply line exposure.
